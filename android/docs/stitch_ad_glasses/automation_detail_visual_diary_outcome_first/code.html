info How it works

Visual Diary utilizes low-power background processing to periodically index your surroundings. Your Personal AI analyzes these captures entirely on-device to build a searchable timeline of significant moments. To ensure complete privacy, data is never sent to the cloud and is strictly pruned according to your designated retention policy.

You can modify these settings at any time.
